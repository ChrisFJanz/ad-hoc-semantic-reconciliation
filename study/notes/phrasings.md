## The observed/judged asymmetry (TBox/ABox vs pragmatics)

*Point:* why the "observed vs judged" layer split is consistent with the lift being a
cognitive act — the cognition for the schematic and instance layers is spent once, at
lift time, and freezes; for pragmatics it cannot freeze and must be re-spent at each use.

> And this is why the observed/judged split still holds and is consistent with all of it.
> The interesting asymmetry is between TBox/ABox and pragmatics after the lift. TBox/ABox
> need cognition once, at lift time; then they freeze cleanly and read as lookup forever.
> Pragmatics can't freeze cleanly — even a finished model "can only describe them, never
> carry them resolved," because they're defeasible and context-relative, so they must be
> re-judged against live context at every use.

*Arose* reconciling the README/§13.9 line "the TBox and ABox are both *observed*: a
determinate lookup any consumer, even a non-cognitive one, can do" against the assertion
that "the lift itself is a cognitive act." The resolution: *observed* is indexed to the
finished lifted model (reading the frozen output of the lift), not to the source data
model, where the layers are implicit and must be recovered — that recovery is the cognition.
(2026-09-14.)
